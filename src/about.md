Aleph-One (ℵ₁) is the consulting practice of **Steven Davey**, based in {{ site.location }}. The work is online.

The name is the first uncountable cardinal — and a nod to the most interesting mathematical fact I know: the set of rational numbers has measure zero. A dense set, and still nothing, if you try to pick a point at random on the line. That tension between what looks abundant and what actually carries weight is a reasonable description of a lot of applied work, including most of what is currently sold as artificial intelligence.

I trained as a mathematician (master’s) and spent the better part of a decade at Standard Bank Group in quantitative risk, technology and data roles — including portfolio management and risk reporting for CIB Risk, an acting CIO assignment for CIB Risk IT during a move from on-premises to cloud, and heading data integration. I left in 2023 after close to eight years, and started this practice.

The work now is to take ideas that are still a bit too abstract — a regulatory document, a modelling approach, a generative-AI capability — and turn them into algorithms that a team can run, inspect and own.
